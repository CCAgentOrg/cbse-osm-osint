📋 The CBSE OSM Tender — Three Rounds, One Winner

Sarthak Sidhant, a 17-year-old student, published a meticulous analysis of CBSE's tender documents on his GitHub blog, exposing how the rules were rewritten across three rounds. His findings were picked up by India Today and Hindustan Times, which both reported extensively on the tender irregularities.

🏛️ The Cross-Party Pattern

What makes this systemic — not partisan — is that the Chary family has operated across both BRS (Telangana) and BJP (Central) governments:

Telangana / BRS Era (2017–2019)

  • Globarena (now Coempt) awarded ₹4.3 crore, 3-year TSBIE contract under KCR's TRS government
  • Norms violated — Globarena didn't meet minimum criteria (5 government universities, state board experience)
  • Contract bypassed Centre for Good Governance
  • 2019: System produced catastrophically wrong results → 23+ student suicides
  • Telangana government eventually pulled the contract

Central / BJP Era (2022–2026)

  • VSN Raju's family joins Coempt board (same day as Prof. S. Sadagopan)
  • CBSE OSM tender won via relaxed third-round bidding
  • BJP leaders Amit Malviya and Gaurav Bhatia defend Coempt: "it works in opposition-ruled states too"
  • 2026: CBSE OSM results disaster — scan errors, blurred marks, mass re-evaluation

The same family. The same company (rebranded). The same exam-tech business. Different political patrons.

Sources: The Hindu, Economic Times, Deccan Chronicle

⚠️ NEET CBT — The Next Target?

On 29 May 2026 — just days after the CBSE OSM scandal broke — the NTA told the Supreme Court that NEET-UG will shift to Computer-Based Test mode from 2027.

The Looming Risk

  • NEET-UG is taken by ~25 lakh students annually
  • Moving to CBT creates the largest single exam-tech procurement in India's history
  • If the pattern holds: TCS will bid → tender may be cancelled → criteria relaxed → smaller firm wins
  • Coempt and/or Eduquity are potential contenders
  • A vendor with their track record handling NEET = catastrophic risk for 25 lakh medical aspirants
